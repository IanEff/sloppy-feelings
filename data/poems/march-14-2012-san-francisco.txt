Walking together     under the moon    with the drunken face    
to the laundromat.        Tio Burbujas    watching over    the tidy piles    
of sheets.        Walking together    toward    the yellow squares of home
on night-soiled streets    in the eternal chill    of March darkness.
Home    in rooms of gems and water    leaves and fur    yellow
yellow wine    in fat glasses.        All the small things    are very clean    
and quiet.        You    I love you    on fresh sheets    under the gentle
window    I am telling you    I loved you    before we even    lived
