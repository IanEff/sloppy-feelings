Walking home    I dreamed    I read    the poems
his ghost wrote. I am not that kind of woman
but    it summons    I answer.        And it lives here
between the door    and the wall.        In my super-hideous
faces and sounds.        It matches my mornings    my
specific blinks.        Oh god.        The darkest dark
I have ever seen    is the rotten ghost    of beloved me
drifting    between fields and buildings    buildings
and buildings.        Lost    and I cut    all the wires.
No reason.
