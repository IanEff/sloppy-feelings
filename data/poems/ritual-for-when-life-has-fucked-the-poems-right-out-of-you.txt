Do not go into your terrible job today.
Sharpen all your pencils
right to the ferrule.
Remember what it smells like
to make words with your hands.
Take a nap with a live coal under your pillow.
Do not take the bus to therapy.
Paint your nails with glitter.
With each stroke, pray to your old self.
With each prayer, remember
a poem that just came out of you,
right into your little notebook.
Remember when you had long hair
long enough to knot around your fist.
Remember your special sadnesses,
your monthly bloody underwear
your poems. Your poems
your poems poems poems.
Is it ever really ok.
