In the rooming-house or what
it really looks like, I am a way of being
naked.       I am a beauty-mark.          The one
in the middle with the holy smile.          You all
have cameras.         How terrific it is to be ready
with a cake with white icing, to be another
year uglier.       Another year decked with fright-
masks & paillettes & to've felled another
leaping stag for the mantle.        I don't know more
than I should.         I don't want to be elderly, to be
in a park.       The Levittown in my head.

She called up & said, shall I come? I said,
you are very kind.      I said, help me
hang the crepe paper flowers reflected
in silver salted butterflies the high forehead
of not-caring.      In the rooming-house parlor,
a funeral for the year.     For every airplane
I have not been on.       We are making airplane noises.
I will dive from great heights
in what will seem a very mythic way.
