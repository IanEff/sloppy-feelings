			for Leigh Stein

Being alive in this world is no longer possible.
The curve is a beckoning line the grace
of the branch nice wet earth & anyway
anything I say is basically grave wax already
perverted by time & distance.              Yes,
drinking makes me sad & also happy & all
I can detect is the spent perfume & sweat
of girls sillage of hopeful disappointment
unwinding all down the sparkling sidewalks.
Where are the animals the only creatures
allowed to swing through the world with
the cruelty of children.       Where is all the music.
Leigh, every day the very best we can do is try
not to be in love.     Try to capture the gestalt
or whatever.        A pain we can predict
starting with the burning ending with the burning
with burning in between.           Or the pretty dresses,
whichever comes first.        The animals, the soft things,
the horns & the horns & the horns & the horns
