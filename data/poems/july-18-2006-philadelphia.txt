I think   the  night    is here.      I drag
after it    polishing every dark tile    with
my sopping hair.      Night hates me    a little
it always brings    the really ugly times
in bars    and alleys    and kitchen floors
and hot tossed sheets    and anywhere I am
just then.        Tonight    I think    I am 
all    better now   secret and small    and 
all all alone    I think
