			for Scott Larner

Remember the Sunday.      All the little good things
freezing in the air. Pressed sound & clarity
You are going to remember how cold preserves memory

How exciting to be heartbroken & glad of it
the new things making everything seem new
the river cracking out under the bridge & the women

Everything will be exactly as you left it, shivering
in space we forgive you don't leave us we forgive you
we're locking the whole city & here's your special key ring
