I am buying morning apples    and thinking
about why    I cannot    love you.        There 
is only    one of you    and     four of me.
All the horns    on Lexington    make a busy song.
Dreaming traffic lights    slow-blinking    in the cold.
In Rhinebeck    I stalked clumsy    like stupid tigers.
I made clouds    with my mouth.        You drew
a bath    I couldn’t take.        I wondered    can I break
my own neck?        Since then    it has been    one long day.        
I promise    I’m getting ready    to be    a person again.
