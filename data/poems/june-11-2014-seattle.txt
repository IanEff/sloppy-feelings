I am homesick tonight    for a place I lived
for two days    three years ago    when I
was someone else.        There were green
ghosts    hiking in the bluffs    showing me
redwoods    finding me jade    in the rising tides.
O mosses    O trees I couldn’t name    O rain
and gas-stations    and washed-out roads
and shivering sadnesses    crumbling down
the Pacific Coast Highway    O library
of extinct seedlings    O    unexist me
