In our own dense forest the leaves blow white & white
& I am sleeping & sleeping under nothing
& I have a bag with a heart in it & I have a bag with an eye in it
& I have a trowel I have a rich little pile & lots to bury
& lots of time & the tree at the center has a man in it
& the man has a small green heart in him
& the heart has a small green vein in it
& the vein has a small green stone in it
in our own dense forest.
