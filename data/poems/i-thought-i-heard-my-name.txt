Be pretty be
the ultraviolet sibling, be
the coyote in the room, be
the fun burial ground, be
the priestess in the
zodiac bath, be
the grasping witch grass
strangling the nettle tree.

Be the only evergreen
trembling on the hillside.
Be marvelous brine, be
the burnt storm bruising the roof, be
scary magic, be
the swan skeleton in the park, be
the echoing bell.
