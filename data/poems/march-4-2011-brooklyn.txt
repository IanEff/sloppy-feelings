Snow smells    like apples and paper.    
Thunder.        We are threading the hours
without wire.        Everything true    becomes
burnished    in the air.        Speed devours
sound.        Your body breaches time.        Me
I am a little bruise    tender behind the knee.
I am quiet in the morning    I come home quiet.
When I press my eyelids    I see a galaxy.
At nights    I am in velvet    sleepless on my horns.
Dreaming is a forest    waking is anemones.
Come home    there are so many things    I have 
to tell you.
