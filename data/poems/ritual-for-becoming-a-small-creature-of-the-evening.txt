Let’s go outside and be loud together.
Get high as ice
and watch air cleave air around your slowing hand.
When it gets dark, make a nest
of hours
and watery sun
and your hair
and the sound the night makes in your chest
and the sound of tires on wet asphalt
and your own held breath
and the feeling of waking up alone, not knowing where you are
and the time when you knew everything would be different from now on
and the last time we spoke on the phone
and
go to sleep there.
Then your heart becomes a bat
circling and circling.
