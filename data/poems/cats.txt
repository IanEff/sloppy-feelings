I am not sure how I became so bad.
I dream of animals who want me
dead.      Did you know birds have teeth?
I am afraid of cats & of falling asleep.
I swear none of these pills are my pills.
I don’t mind being sad because being sad
means I am still alive in the world.     You know,
you are never alone—I don’t mean that
in a kindly way.     All these things caught
in ambered plastic—they are going
to save us from the black the talons
the sadness the petting zoos
