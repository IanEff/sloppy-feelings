Palo santo    sacred tree    when I am    High Priestess
I will make it    into a coffee table    and do drugs
on its surface.        Yes    I am crazy    crazier    than god.
Have you noticed    sadness    rolls    downhill?
In the Botanica de los Suenos    nothing but    murky oils
and dry bundles    rustling    without moving.        Why 
is my brain    working hard    to destroy me.        These hills
folded and divine    the cards    turning over    and over
don’t mean change.        Change means life.    Life means death.
