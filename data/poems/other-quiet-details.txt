			after Annie Dillard

I consider there should have been an elm outside your window.              I would
like to kick out your chair.         I am married to a line of ever farther point.
I have a habit & a watch & a terrible way of using the telephone.            I know
in other places they are just waking upside-down over the desert sand out east
over the river which I couldn’t see you are thinking of it a closed cipher.         I
know in other places they are finding things on streets I would list them the
scallops & folded notes the dirt & how I should not touch them the castellated
cities the total bonjour of lifting something out of gutter like rust if rust had
been pretty it was like a moon in there dark & smaller than I thought but
surely the really interesting part is how tenderly on window ledges ugly things
alight.
