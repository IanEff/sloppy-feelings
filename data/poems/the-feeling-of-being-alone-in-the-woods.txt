			for Molly Dorozenski

Night then night. Your name’s the call.
Crushed balsam tiny fear of tiny death.
Darling of occidental evening I am with you.
Even when you are alone. Even night.        You
were born then I was born then the forest we
are the long low note the signal of proximity
I love you firbank I love you mosscroft
I love you light through leaves.   Darling of
Darling of everything fragrant & alive.
