Now we are all used up
on Champagne and bad thoughts.
Back then, when I was me
I had no fucking idea.
Now I am sad on pills
and too much noise.
It is easy to be sad here
there are so many places
for my sadness to go.

Pink tiger, why is beauty
strange and why in dreams
do only bad things happen?
Last night in sleep
all the wonder.
Oh wow, we were glowing,
sparklers alive in our guts.
I swear to god you cannot buy
this kind of shame.

I am counting down to a time
when we will be ok.

I would give you anything
to take me back
to the time before everything.
The long forever where
it all belonged to us.
I promise I will never
leave us again.
