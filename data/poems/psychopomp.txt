Unfolding
over the bridge
I can see
up
my own dress.

How long
will this long sadness
lope after me
shaking the boards
collecting my nosebleeds
making nests
of my hair?

Not presence
but the absence
of absence.

All this vulvic divinity
discharge of poems
birthright of being
a lady
or whatever
I don’t want it.
How many men
have chased me
away from myself?
Well
I never asked
for that.

Upside down
over the water
bats argue
about darkness
bats take
the flavor of fruit
from my very
mouth.

Soft crescent of moon
softens in the current.
I can only ever
hide
inside the softness
of my own
soft palm.

Inside out
my dress drying
on the rails
the seams like the spancel
yoking me
to earth.

To be naked
outside
is to be the most
dead
psychopomp leading
my own self
through the trees.


How did we all get here.
I don’t know
and anyway
I’m not
telling.
