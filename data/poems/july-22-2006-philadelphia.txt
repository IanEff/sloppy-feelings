Death smells    like a handful of nickels.        
We see her    everywhere    when we are ready 
to close    our eyes.    We see    her in the doorway    
irises bright and too large    she is by the bookshelf    
then the edge    of the bed.        Crying in the kitchen
half-angry    half-alive    in the blue light   of city sky.    
We will forgive her    If she will forgive us.
