Your coat exhales    the last of winter    you are my husband
you put on your glasses    you are    my other    husband.
You take a walk    in the long night    you are my other    other 
husband.        I can’t think    with spring    edging in    so slow and cold.
I stand naked    in the kitchen    pitting dates    with my mouth.
In warmer other places    date trees    are pollinated    by hand.
Even the flowers are edible.        Here    bees make fennel.
Moths make  dust.    Birds make shit.    Bats make tequila. 
Winds make amaranth.        You make    poems    in your sleep.
