It is snowing & you are sad & everything
is waiting for something.       Can I be
your harp now?          Your familiar smell.
Your nice place to go on a Wednesday night.
Remember the eastern avenues.           Under snow
they are gentle & alive soft with grassy light
& muffled desire.        The trains are moving
& I can see your veins.       You know we love you.
You know everyone is scared.          Everything is okay.
I have found out for you the secret to youth
& no more crying ever.        It is to be arrogant
& beautiful.      It is too much blush.
