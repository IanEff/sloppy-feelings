How does it feel
to be a spell?

Unborn, reddish
it’s about half a thought or
like the edge of a dream
I don’t know
what am I supposed to feel?

In my last three lives
I was a kitten
little sad-faced cat
slipping between worlds.

I ate a black bee
a yellow bee
a red bat
an orange bat.

I’m assuming that this
is how I died.
Little bat-faced kitten
nuzzling a ghost.
