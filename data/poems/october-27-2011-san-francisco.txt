I am homesick    for everyone.        I’m crying
real tears    onto evening’s    dampening    t-shirt.        
When you     leave me    I believe you.        Like warnings    
like the clouds.        I can’t remember    kissing    
anyone    other    than you    all those    retreating 
faces    sorry    behind    old moons    I’m waxing
gibbous    about    to go in.        You know    I would rather     
feel good    than ever    write     another poem    ever    again
