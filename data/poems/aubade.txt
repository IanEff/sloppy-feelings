Of course the morning is a knife. it isn't a lie
like a good to be home. like I am on my way.
& it skims dehuman streets. ice from ice.
Breathless, I am all, remember this & that?
& you're all, no. Really it comes down
to various gilded glasses heavy crystal
splinters. glorious whiskeys. The moon is
still out again. but here I am. anyway.
walking fast. & gloveless.
