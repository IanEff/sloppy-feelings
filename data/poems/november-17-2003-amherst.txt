The trees bend to me    if sleeping is knowing
and knowing    is something terrible    and wrong. 
The tree parts    to love the telephone wires    and 
words fall out    and then     and then no one knows.
I ride the wires    on the disconnected voice    as a star 
dips    not last or first    to you as though    although    
I may have once been   beautiful.        Still    you rail    
from the bed     as trees bend.        As I said    I am 
a ghost    slender word     on a wire   that does not 
reach    it falls    out or you are not home
