Since 1996    I have not    written a poem
I haven’t    said something I meant    or
slept under    anything clean.        Once
with street lights    for a necklace    and basalt
in my mouth    I had a city.        I had a grave
and a beautiful city.     My god    he lived
in the city    and so    I burned   it down.
in 1996    I was so pretty    I was     kissing
in a park    in the shadow    of a highway
and the fake mountain    and    the curious geese.
