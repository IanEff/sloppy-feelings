			                         for Ian Furst

I'm so happy I can't write.
There are great waters moving underground.
Think of the mollusks crackling in their shells.
Now think of a number between one & ten.
I love you so much I am sick in bed for days.
The avenues are breathing like animals.
Me, I am a bruise, tender & plum-yellow.
You & I should drink fairly heavily tonight.
Thank you for the cigarettes.
Then what happened?
Mushrooms bloom in the circle of earth.
We run very fast & far.
We never thirst or tire.
