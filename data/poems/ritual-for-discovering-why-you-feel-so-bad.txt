Walk up the long hill.
Do not cry until I tell you you can cry.
Ok.
You can cry.
The sun rises later and later from its fulcrum of bruisy red.
Golden light for everyone but you
and all those windows beginning to warm in the early dark.
Don’t let the beauty of the city comfort you.
Where the mountains meet the water meet the streets is not for you.
This dusting of snow won’t last.
Winter will pull wet clouds over the smoky end of day.
Do you believe any of this?
No?
Go back to sleep alone and remember what I have told you
and remember the truth you made for yourself and no one else.
