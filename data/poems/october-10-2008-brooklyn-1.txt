The salt tracks.    We follow them    out    and down.       What if every day    
is Sunday    guttering out    greasy ash    the color    of things    that grow 
in the dark    slender    singular    thwarted scent     and sugared top.        
I am going    to stand here.        I am going to stand here.        I am going 
to stand here    until I die    making birds     on the wall     with my hands.
