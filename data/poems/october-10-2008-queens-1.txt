Inside    the chambers    echoing light    comes to me    bats shake   
the surface    of thought.        Spindly castles of sand    terrific fulgurites    
I make    with my terrific eyes    still smoking.        If I lay my head    
on your chest    you could die.        I will say    these things   I will do     
these things    in this order    and    you will be safe.        Forever.
