The unbundled morning    spills dirt    and leaves.
People sleep    in unquiet beds    we’ve seen them
not answer their doors    it’s strange.        That’s 
not death.        Between our fingers is salt    salt
is a mineral    on a specially illuminated page.  
People like dictionaries    sugar    beads    shoes
women    other feathered things.        They bathe
in the evenings.        They have    found it.
That’s not death.       That is.
