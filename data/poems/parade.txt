In empire’s naughty pants. the city wiggles. its fingers.
I’ve said: come down from there. I’ve said: brush off
the pigeon skull. company’s coming. & what
a company it was. the princes. the monarchs.
the swallowtails. the bitches. we were a parade.
down winter’s avenue. I was gap-toothed. I was tasty
nasturtium. this year is cancelled. the dowager folds
her dusty legs. around your waist. the sun never sets
as I’ve said. on ponderous tortures. your pecten.
your mitre. keeps the rain out. & the bishop in.
I have something. to tell. throw down. the rope.
If this year happened. we’d be tight & shiny. we’d spank the maid.
