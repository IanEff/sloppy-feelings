Halfway up the mountain          over the coverlet    gentle    sad

fragrant skin.        Naked in the quiet mink.         Animals are gentle & sad

all over my shoulders.          Nice warm hair.        Being lonely is extravagant

like sick home from work         only no one asks about it.


I'm more tired than I've ever been.           When I'm tired I'm just like the birch-tree

only on fire     with thoughts.         Rustle & flutter.      Do you think of me

sometimes.       I do.      It's raining today but the sound-man is not making sounds

no ticking radiator      it's lonely.     No one plays      anymore.     I am a grown-up.

Only I am better now         for personal reasons.
