It’s not the hyacinth    it’s the air    around it.    The heads 
fall as I watch    coughing up clouds    of pollen    tincture of    
snow    elegant vapors    gentle mist   of vomit    a Monday
sick    of cold    and mirrors.        This branch doesn’t snap
it sways    we are breathing slow    swallowing iron    I am
holding    the firm cool edge of the firm cool world.
I miss you.        In my dreams    I wrestled a bobcat.      
I texted a slavering wolf    I leapt away    from the dancing 
sparking wire.        Roots in water    excrement of crow
I haven’t    shat    in days
