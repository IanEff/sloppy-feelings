The scale of the body    is musical and reclining.        Black soot
black burned-out morning    the ring a lidless eye.        Hollowed
clean rooms    chilled bed sheets.        When it happened    I became
someone else    you became    you.        I’m a frightened hare    long
scream    tired folds    of the tired dead face    the chained fox
snapping    at cold new snow.        God is a parrot    on fire    useless
and beautiful.        Your mouth’s unfinished ribbon    can’t forget that day.
