Mottled sea    and mouldering leaf    gentle Atlantic colors
washing gentle    over the times    and the times    what
makes you    think    autumn is ahead of us    what makes
you laugh    from the future.    Water   pours through
the chasm    angry nighttime swim    celadon and changing 
jade    changeable tides.        Touch me    here    here    and here
it    all     comes back    just not     when you     call it.
