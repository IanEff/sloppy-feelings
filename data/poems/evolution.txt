The aggressive horns of the snail rip screen from screen
or is it the construction?     This morning something
half-man, half-hare struggles free of its channeled case.
We watch it pace in the alley awkward
with its curling tail.    In the afternoon it calls up
through the rented screen: sir, you were as beautiful
as race-car drivers.     Madame, you are a fool.       We call it
a moth & put it in a jar because we are not kind.
