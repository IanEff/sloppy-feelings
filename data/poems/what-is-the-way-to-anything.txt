Space
I can’t think of it
wide wild darkness
coiled in the ear like thought.
The haze of gorgeous sleep
but for like a billion years.

When it’s all over it will be
the hungry sound of paper tearing.
Like sucking a penny.
Like going into the cold morning
with damp hair and sleep-creased skin.

When the heater clicks on
that is the exact temperature
of the universe, the exact scent
the toasted smell of dust.
Click click click.
I’m going to go ahead and
choose the next color of the cosmos.

In one galaxy it is five o'clock
in the evening on a Northeast November
and in another
I never have to be.
