			                       for Molly Dorozenski, Doug Rice, & Jason Daniel Schwartz

O friends.      I’m sorry for everything & for time.
Remember when we all lived on the same street
& the time when the time when the times O
I forget.     We drink to refuse the Future.
We dance to reassemble made-up times.
My heart is green your hearts are purple & blue
we are a driftwood fire dying out on the edges
of all the beaches.     We do endless lines
of ghost tears.     Nothing has ever been so funny.
