I boiled an egg    I did    a really good job.
Bubbles move    upward    that’s science.
In the cold kitchen    it is snowing    a tiny 
and gentle snow    that’s loneliness.

You’re probably dead.        I’m not 
very old    but I’m old enough    to know
what it feels like    to be married    today
