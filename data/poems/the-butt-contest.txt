			for Molly Dorozenski

Have you ever seen dancing like our kind of dancing.
No.      You are on a boat tar & salt & hemp.
Even when your shoes are ugly I love you the most.
Even when you belong to birds I am hoping.
Molly, when you are away there is too much doughnut.
Bad people pissing all over Belinda Carlisle.
The bridges wilt.     Oysters go extinct.       Nothing’s fair.
Come home to me.          Add your sweet Gs
to all my cranky fuckins.     I can’t eat my rabbit
until you come home & tell me that drusy is the face of God.
