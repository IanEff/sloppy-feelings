All of my poems    are dead poems
backlit    panoramic    hell-bent.
I whistle    the night    to death
snap pop of stars    out like bulbs
one by one    by one the houses
drift past    clouds of gnats    fizzing
cumulus    with secret heat    above
maternal hills    pregnant and dangerous
with luminous coal.        Oh Tennessee
red-walled and ornate    I am inside
the poem    under the lake and glowing
phosphorus and wrong    take it back
oh please    take it back
