Take off your dress
the one with the slavering wolf heads.
Let them go hungry tonight.

In the next room, your husband
is so thirsty, drinking
his own heart’s blood
in warm, salty draughts.

Stand up on your hind legs.
With your sorry tongue, lick
the blood from his cheek.
Swap the heart for an orange.
He won’t notice.
He didn’t make your destroying sadness.

Allow yourself to soften around him.
Fold him in your hair.
Feed him your grief.
You made it yourself
with your own two paws.
